# retestR 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* Expected-gain model for repeaters fitted by marginal ML, robust to preknowledge (`rt_fit()`).
* Posterior-predictive evidence: score gain, exposed-item performance, response speed (`rt_evidence()`).
* Risk index calibrated by parametric bootstrap with explicit false-positive rates (`rt_risk()`).
* Raw-gain baseline (`rt_raw_gain()`) and known-truth simulation (`rt_simulate()`).
